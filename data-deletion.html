NARA CORE allows people to request deletion of locally stored Instagram-related data associated with their interactions with the authorized Instagram account.

How to request deletion

  1. Email salamis4rah@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line NARA CORE Data Deletion Request.
  3. Include the Instagram username associated with the data you want deleted.
  4. Briefly describe the type of data or interaction involved, such as direct messages or comments, so the relevant records can be located.

Verification

We may ask for reasonable information to verify that the requester controls the relevant Instagram account or is otherwise authorized to make the request. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or other authentication secrets.

What will be deleted

After a valid request is verified, we will make reasonable efforts to remove matching locally stored operational records that are no longer required for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, or platform compliance.

Timing

Verified deletion requests will normally be completed within 30 days. If additional time is required, the requester will be informed where reasonably possible.

Data held by Meta

This deletion process applies to data stored by NARA CORE. Data held independently by Instagram or Meta is governed by Meta's own products, controls, and policies.

Contact

Deletion requests and questions may be sent to salamis4rah@gmail.com.
