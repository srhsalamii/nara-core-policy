This Privacy Policy explains how NARA CORE ("NARA CORE", "the app", "we", or "us") processes information when the app is connected to an Instagram Professional account through Meta's official Instagram APIs.

1. Scope

NARA CORE is currently a private, owner-operated application. It is designed to work only with Instagram accounts that have been explicitly authorized by the account owner or administrator.

2. Information we may process

Depending on the Instagram permissions granted to the app and the activity received through Meta's APIs, NARA CORE may process:

  • Instagram account identifiers and usernames;
  • comments and direct-message content sent to or associated with the authorized Instagram account;
  • metadata related to those interactions, such as event type, timestamps, sender identifiers, media or conversation identifiers, and delivery metadata;
  • OAuth access credentials and related token metadata required to maintain the authorized connection; and
  • technical and security logs needed to operate, troubleshoot, and protect the app.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to operate NARA CORE for the authorized Instagram account, including receiving Instagram events, organizing or analyzing incoming interactions, maintaining continuity, and—only when explicitly enabled by the owner—preparing or sending permitted responses or content through Meta's APIs.

4. Security and trust handling

External Instagram messages and comments are treated as untrusted external input. NARA CORE separates external content from internal instructions and does not grant external messages system-level authority.

Authentication secrets and access tokens are stored in protected credential storage on the owner's system rather than intentionally exposed in public logs or policy pages.

5. Storage and retention

NARA CORE may store normalized operational event records locally for continuity, testing, debugging, and application operation. Raw webhook request bodies are not intentionally retained as a permanent record. Data is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for these purposes, unless a longer retention period is required for security, legal, or compliance reasons.

6. Sharing and sale of data

NARA CORE does not sell personal information. The app does not provide personal information to advertisers. Information may be transmitted to Meta as required to use Instagram's official APIs, and may be processed by infrastructure or service providers strictly as necessary to operate the app.

7. User choices and deletion

A person whose Instagram interaction data has been processed by NARA CORE may request deletion of locally stored data associated with their Instagram account. Instructions are available on the Data Deletion page.

8. Children's privacy

NARA CORE is not intentionally designed to collect information from children. If we learn that information was collected in a way that requires deletion under applicable law, we will take reasonable steps to remove it.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when NARA CORE's functionality, data practices, or legal requirements change. The effective date shown at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
